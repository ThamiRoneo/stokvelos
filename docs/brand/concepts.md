# StokvelOS — Round 2 Concepts

- **R2-A / concept-d "Circular ledger":** A circle of ledger ruling lines with one marked position (current payout turn), reading as a rotating ledger at any age.
- **R2-B / concept-e "Refined ledger":** Three bold ruled lines with one highlighted payout column and clear negative space, plus a subtle cycle cue.
- **R2-C / concept-f "S-ledger monogram":** A bold "S" whose middle stroke is a ledger line carrying a payout dot.
- **R2-B·S / concept-g "S-ledger refined":** Bold "S" whose strokes are ledger rules, with a payout column and cycle arc cut in as gaps (mask). Works at 16px, one colour, reversed.
