# StokvelOS — Brand Research

## Round 1 (2026-11-23 10:59)
Concepts: A monogram "S", B cycle ring (abstract ring + dot), C ledger (3 ruled lines + highlighted column).
User liked B and C but B's abstract ring+dot risked being misunderstood by 40–50 year-olds.
Checkpoint paused; no kit built.

## Round 2 update — clarity refactor
Goal: merge literal/readability with distinctiveness; work at 16px, one colour, reversed.
Constraints: no arrows, coins, money bags, shields, globes; simple geometry.

Concepts (one sentence each):
- R2-A "Circular ledger": a circle of ledger ruling lines with one marked position (current payout turn), reading as a rotating ledger at any age.
- R2-B "Refined ledger": three bold ruled lines with one highlighted payout column and clear negative space, plus a subtle cycle cue.
- R2-C "S-ledger monogram": a bold "S" whose middle stroke is a ledger line carrying a payout dot.

Recommendation: R2-A (most universally legible merge of rotation + ledger), with R2-C as strongest secondary (ownership initial).
